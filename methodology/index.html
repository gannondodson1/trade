How the record is made

Only what has already happened, and only after the delay rules pass, appears here.

Public pages read a published record only. Letters print when there is something worth saying. We do not publish the next trade, the live book, account numbers, or the owner’s name.

NAV is the last delayed mark in the public log. Losses stay in the record. We do not rewrite them.

Not trading advice. You can lose money. These numbers are from after the close, not live.
