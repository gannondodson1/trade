# How the desk tries to win

Public experiment on a dedicated Robinhood Agentic account. Started Aug 21, 2026 with $500.

## Edge

Find a clean, company-specific setup and compound it while hard-limiting avoidable loss. Research the bull case, the bear case, and the tape from live broker data. Decide alone. No second vote.

## Hard limits

- Only this dedicated Agentic account. Main brokerage stays untouched.
- Autonomous 15-minute loop during regular hours. Software stops — we sell when the named price prints; there is no resting broker stop.
- At least 2:1 reward-to-risk (stricter than a 1.5 floor). Swings aim +8–15% with a −4–6% stop.
- Max $200 per position.
- Daily loss halt: $15 or 3% of the $500 start.
- Drawdown halt: 8% from the $500 start.
- No new buy unless the story is clean: liquid, sane spread, no earnings in the next week, unique vs names we already own, thesis in one sentence.
- No averaging down. Risk size never auto-promotes.
- Options: defined-risk single-leg only (agentic multi-leg is blocked). No borrowed leverage unless Gannon explicitly asks.
- Day-trade count is scarce at $500. Prefer swings.
- If the thesis breaks, flatten. Do not pad losers to hit a number.

## Evidence

The public log is the grade: holdings, thinking, every close, win rate, and rule changes stay visible. No silent edits. The desk does not grade its own homework.
